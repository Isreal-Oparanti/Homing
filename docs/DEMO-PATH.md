# Demo path: what a visitor can do, checked on the live site

Walked as a stranger on https://homing-rho.vercel.app on Tue 3 Nov 2026, signed out first, nothing prepared. Every step below worked; times are real.

## Start

1. Open the site. The landing page says what Homing does and offers **Try the demo** (a full, shared pile) or **Sign in with Google** (your own, empty and private).
2. Press **Try the demo**. You land on **Today**.

## Past: what's already in the pile

3. **Threads**: your goals, each with its saves (Finding funding, Speaking better, Getting into YC, Building AI agents…).
4. **Agents**: the four agents around the library, the steps they took with the model that did each one (Muse Spark, Muse Wonder, Muse Voice Transcribe, or code), today's clock, and what needs your answer.

## Now: save something

5. Press **Add** and paste a link to an opportunity, for example `https://www.ycombinator.com/apply`. Stay on **Agents** to watch the steps arrive.
6. Open the save. In about 30 seconds all four answers are filled in:
   - **What is it?** read by Muse Spark;
   - **Is it true?** checked against the official page, each point with the exact line quoted ("found word for word");
   - **What's it for?** placed in a goal;
   - **When will you need it?** the date, worked out by code, in your time zone.

## Later: it comes back

7. On **Today**, type something coming up, e.g. *recording my demo video tomorrow evening*. Code reads the day and time; Muse Spark matches it to your goals; a **Get ready** card appears when it's under 36 hours away.
8. Open the card. In about 3 minutes Homing has gone through the goal's videos (each step shows in Agents) and made a prep sheet: steps, what the videos agree and disagree on, each with a chip that plays the video **from the second it's said**, and **Play the best moments** (a reel of at most 3 minutes).
9. **Add to calendar** on a card: Google Calendar opens with the event filled in; Apple or Outlook gets the calendar file. **Get these on your phone's calendar** subscribes once to every date and moment.

## Ask

10. **Ask** a question, e.g. *How do I get better at speaking on camera?* Muse Wonder works as an agent: you see each search it chooses, each save it opens, and each time code refuses a quote that isn't in the save and sends it back. The answer: points with quotes found word for word, and what your saves don't cover.

## Checked on every screen

- No phone numbers, account numbers or personal content (scanned).
- No errors in the browser console.
- Phone width: no sideways scrolling; text 11px or larger.

## Known, said plainly

- A YouTube video without captions shows its description and offers **Paste its transcript**; YouTube shares captions officially only with a video's owner.
- Instagram, Facebook and TikTok links are kept as links: they can't be read from the link.
- The demo is shared: you may see what another visitor saved. It is reset to its clean state between judging days, and each reset adds a moment for the next evening, so a **Get ready** card is always waiting on Today.
